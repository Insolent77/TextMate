# Third-party notices

## LibreOffice Russian dictionary (`ru_RU`)

TextMate's **No AI — Russian** mode downloads and locally caches the Russian
Hunspell dictionary maintained by the LibreOffice dictionaries project.

Source:
https://github.com/LibreOffice/dictionaries/tree/master/ru_RU

The authoritative license terms are available in the upstream `ru_RU/LICENSE`
file.

TextMate downloads only `.aff` and `.dic` dictionary data. It does not download
or execute remote JavaScript for spellchecking.
